# BrightTV Viewership Analytics

## Business Analytics Case Study

BrightTV Viewership Analytics is a business analytics case study focused on understanding viewing behaviour and identifying opportunities to strengthen content consumption and support user-base growth.

## Business Objective

BrightTV's CEO wants to grow the company's subscription base during the current financial year.

The analysis focuses on understanding viewing trends, consumption drivers, low-consumption periods, audience opportunities and potential programming initiatives.## Analytical Approach

### Excel — Overall Viewership

Established the overall BrightTV viewing picture across audience, volume, duration, channels and viewing periods.

The analysis established the strongest viewing segments, highest and lowest consumption periods, and overall channel performance.

### Data Studio — Audience Intelligence

Established who is watching BrightTV and how viewing activity differs across demographic and geographic segments.

The analysis highlighted audience concentration, representation and potential under-served segments.

### Power BI — Performance Gaps

Identified where BrightTV's viewing performance is weakest and where opportunities exist across periods, channels, engagement and markets.

The analysis highlighted underperforming days and time periods, channel concentration, engagement differences and audience gaps.

### Databricks — Viewing Behaviour

Established how viewing behaviour varies through session duration, frequency, content and time of day.

The analysis identified stronger engagement patterns, high-usage sessions and content associated with longer viewing.

### Lovable — Strategic Response

Translated the analytical findings into targeted programming, content and market opportunities.

The final solution focuses on testing high-engagement content in weaker periods, strengthening proven content, and targeting under-served audiences and markets.
